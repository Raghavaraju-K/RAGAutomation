CASH FLOW AND REPAYMENT SOURCES (UK)
Primary repayment source is operating cash flow after HMRC and VAT. Secondary source is collateral realisation under debenture. Tertiary source is guarantor support.
Free Cash Flow equals Operating Cash Flow minus Maintenance Capex minus Dividends. Must be positive for term loan approval.
Revolving credit repayment depends on working capital cycle: receivables days plus inventory days minus payables days. Cash conversion cycle above 90 days needs larger revolver headroom.
Stress test: 20 percent EBITDA decline must still keep DSCR above 1.0x, and Bank of England base rate plus 3 percent must still be affordable. If not, reduce tenor or require amortisation.
Seasonal borrowers need peak-trough analysis with monthly management accounts.
