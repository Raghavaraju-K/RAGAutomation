INDUSTRY AND MARKET RISK (UK)
Industry outlook scored: Strong, Stable, Cyclical, Declining. Declining industries (coal, high-street retail) require higher DSCR 1.50x and lower leverage 2.5x.
Customer concentration: top 3 customers above 40 percent revenue is high risk. Mitigant: assignment of contracts, diversification plan.
Supplier concentration: single supplier above 50 percent is high risk. Mitigant: alternative sourcing letter.
UK regulatory risk: FCA-regulated, gambling, crypto, defence borrowers need enhanced due diligence, OFSI screening, and compliance officer sign-off.
ESG and climate: borrowers with adverse environmental findings need remediation plan and PRA climate-risk assessment with reserve before funding.
