FINANCIAL STATEMENT SPREADING AND ANALYSIS (UK)
Spread 3 years of audited financials under UK GAAP or IFRS plus interim management accounts. Normalise for non-recurring items: asset sales, goodwill impairment, restructuring charges.
Key ratios: Current Ratio equals Current Assets divided by Current Liabilities. Minimum 1.20x for working capital borrowers.
Leverage: Debt to EBITDA must be below 3.5x for senior unsecured, below 4.5x for leveraged borrowers with sponsor support.
Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) equals EBITDA divided by Interest plus Current Portion Long Term Debt, tested on post-tax cash flow after HMRC and VAT. Minimum DSCR is 1.20x. Below 1.10x requires decline or restructuring.
EBITDA margin below 10 percent signals weak profitability for manufacturing borrowers.
Trend: declining revenue for two consecutive years is a negative flag requiring explanation in the risk section with Companies House filing cross-check.
