CREDIT MEMORANDUM OVERVIEW (UK)
A UK credit memorandum (credit memo) is the formal document a lender uses to request credit approval under FCA and PRA standards.
UK Sections: Executive Summary, Borrower Background (with Companies House number), Purpose of Facility, Financial Analysis, Repayment Sources, Collateral Analysis (debenture, fixed and floating charges), Risk Assessment and Mitigants, IFRS 9 Stage and Provisions, Consumer Duty and Fair Value Assessment, Covenants and Conditions, Recommendation.
The executive summary states borrower name, facility amount in pounds sterling, facility type (term loan, revolving credit, overdraft, buy-to-let mortgage), tenor, SONIA-based pricing, purpose, and recommendation (approve or decline).
The recommendation must be supported by affordability and cash flow coverage, collateral coverage, covenant compliance, and Consumer Duty good outcomes.
