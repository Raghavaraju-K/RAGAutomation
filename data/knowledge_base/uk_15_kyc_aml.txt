UK KYC AML GDPR AND COMPANIES HOUSE
UK financial crime compliance follows the Money Laundering Regulations 2017 (MLR 2017), Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (POCA), and JMLSG guidance. Customer Due Diligence (CDD): verify identity via Companies House, electoral roll, HMRC records. Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) for high-risk countries, politically exposed persons (PEPs), complex structures.
Sanctions screening against OFSI consolidated list. Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs) to the National Crime Agency (NCA) with DAML consent where needed. Companies House charge registration within 21 days of creation; confirm debenture, personal guarantee, and negative pledge.
Data protection under UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018: lawful basis, data minimisation, 6-year retention for loan files, Subject Access Requests within 1 month. Credit memo must cite lawful basis and Companies House number.
