UK CONDUCT VULNERABILITY ARREARS AND FORBEARANCE
FCA Consumer Duty and FG21/1 on vulnerable customers: identify health, life events, resilience, capability drivers. Tailor collections: Breathing Space (Debt Respite Scheme) gives 60 days enforcement pause. FCA CONC 7 requires forbearance before repossession: payment plan, reduced payments, term extension.
Arrears handling: 15-day arrears letter, 30-day phone contact, 60-day home-visit or enhanced review, 90-day litigation review. Repossession is last resort per MCOB 13 and pre-action protocol. Every credit memo for customers in arrears must show arrears history, forbearance already granted, and customer outcome statement.
Complaints follow DISP: resolve within 8 weeks, inform of Financial Ombudsman Service (FOS). No fees that conflict with fair value under Consumer Duty price and value outcome.
